# M5.1-operator-attention — the record prompts, not just remembers

**Status**: Completed (authored, accepted and built 2026-07-07, operator-directed in session).
**Sits at**: Sub-milestone of M5-backfill on the sequence axis — hardening surfaced by M5's own close-out, not new capability. Its T3s land in T2-ontology (the `verification.deferred` event, schema 0.5.0) and T2-projection (the attention surfaces).

---

## 1. Why this sub-milestone

Two failure modes surfaced while closing M4/M5, both of the same species: **the record knew, but nobody was prompted.**

1. **The ceremony-prompting gap** (inbox `2026-07-03.ceremony-prompting-gap`): ceremonies are enforced only lazily — the draft gate blocks implementation-on-draft, but nothing *prompts* a pending ceremony. `T3-autonomous-extractor` sat authored-but-unaccepted for 23 days while the operator believed it built. At M5.1 authoring the log held two more live instances: `T2-ingest` draft for 69 commits, and `M5-backfill` with all scheduled T3s closed but no closure.
2. **The skipped-vs-deferred conflation**: M4/M5's operator legs (exfu.ai upload, Cowork verification, the rehearsal run) are recorded as `verification.skipped` — indistinguishable from "not doing it". The intent — *come back to this* — was held in the operator's head, which is exactly what the tracker exists to replace (T1 §3.3).

The fix is one capability: **queues of things only the operator can move, surfaced by the projections rather than discovered by accident.**

## 2. How — T3 tasks

1. **`T3-verification-deferred`** — under T2-ontology: the `verification.deferred` event (schema epoch 0.5.0) — "skipped for pragmatic reasons, temporarily; we mean to return". Open until a later `verification.*` event on the same entity resolves it.
2. **`T3-pending-ceremony-surfacing`** — under T2-projection: the `attention` projection block, the summary's *Awaiting operator* section, the `pending-ceremony` and `deferred-verification` gate warns, and inbox age annotations.

## 3. Definition of done

- Schema `0.5.0` lands; the existing log validates unmigrated; a `verification.deferred` event validates and a reason-less one is rejected.
- The gate warns (never blocks) on: draft plans awaiting acceptance, live milestones with all scheduled T3s closed, and open deferrals; a resolved deferral goes quiet. Fixture-tested (`tests/gate/fixture-attention/`).
- `summary.md` carries the *Awaiting operator* section and inbox items show their untriaged age.
- Dogfood proof: the real log's own gaps (`T2-ingest` draft, `M5-backfill` closure) surface in the summary and gate output of the delivering commit.

## 4. Out of scope

- Session-orientation counts — `session-orient.sh`'s contract is fingerprint-only, no cache reads; the prompting surfaces are the summary (regenerated by every repack) and the gate (fired at every push/merge). Revisit only if those prove insufficient.
- Auto-acceptance or any weakening of operator-only ceremonies — this milestone prompts them, never performs them.
- Retro-classifying existing `verification.skipped` events as deferred — append-only; new deferrals use the new event, old skips stand as written.
